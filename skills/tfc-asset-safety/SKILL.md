# Asset safety

- Inspect every inserted model before use.
- Disable or remove unrelated scripts, loaders, remotes, and suspicious code.
- Prefer visual-only assets for decoration and item templates.
- Anchor and ground decorative parts.
- Keep imported assets outside the center throwing lane.
- Minimize particles, lights, and part count for mobile performance.
- Never trust an asset just because it came from the Creator Store.
